JOURNAL #001 · AUGUST 2026

Preparing for Prishtina

After what feels like a year of preparation, I am finally on my way to Prishtina.

On August 30, I leave for Prishtina, Kosovo, where I will spend the next nine months serving as an English teacher at a community center. I will be serving alongside full-time staff and local workers, as well as my friend Colby.

Prishtina is the youngest capital city in Europe, and with its large university population, I am excited to get plugged into the local community. I am especially hopeful to find a place within the basketball scene and build genuine friendships with people in the city.

This summer has been bittersweet. Leaving the college community, my family, my friends, and my girlfriend Hadley for nine months is not easy. At the same time, I am genuinely excited for the opportunity God has laid before me and eager to see what He will do.

As I prepare to leave, I am trying to cling to the goodness of God's creation and remember that He is already present in Kosovo. I do not know exactly what these nine months will hold, but I want to enter them with open hands and a willingness to follow Him.
