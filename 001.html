Welcome to my monthly newsletter for my time in Kosovo! I hope this newsletter will provide a way for you to follow along with my experience, and a way for you to know how you can pray and support me in this mission.

I am grateful for the community that is behind me as I go, and pray that you would be able to draw nearer to Christ through your support of me!

Preparing for Prishtina

What feels like a year of preparation is about to come to fruition, when I leave for Prishtina, Kosovo on August 30th. When I arrive, I will get to serve as an English teacher at a community center in Prishtina alongside a team of full-time staff and locals. I will also be serving with my friend Colby (pictured above).

Prishtina is the youngest capital city in all of Europe, and has several universities, meaning there will be plenty of opportunities to get plugged into the local community (and hopefully the local basketball scene).

It has been a bittersweet summer in preparation, saying goodbye to my college community and moving on to this next chapter of life. It will certainly be challenging leaving family, friends, and my girlfriend, Hadley, for 9 months. However, I am so excited for this opportunity that God has laid before me, and I feel very ready to be molded and shaped by my time in Kosovo.

I have been clinging to the goodness of God’s creation as I prepare to move to another beautiful place under Christ’s dominion.

Prayer Requests

  • Safe flights: Dulles → Vienna → Prishtina — August 30–31
  • Limited culture shock: It can be overwhelming entering a new place; pray for peace as I settle in.
  • Team cohesion: It is important to be close with those I serve with; pray we would come together under Christ.

Thank you so much for supporting me both financially and in prayer in these past months of preparation for my departure.
