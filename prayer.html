PRAYER REQUESTS

Pray with me.

These are the things I am asking the Lord to grow in me and around me during my time in Kosovo.

01

Deeper Cultural Immersion

Pray that I would grow in my understanding of Kosovo and its people through learning Albanian and seeking out cultural experiences. I want to move beyond simply living in Kosovo and grow in my understanding of what it means to be a Kosovar.

02

Peace of Mind

Culture shock often sets in after the initial “honeymoon phase.” Pray that my mind would be continually renewed by Christ, rather than becoming tangled in the challenges of adjusting to a new culture.

03

Relationships with Locals

Pray that I would build genuine friendships with people in Prishtina. I hope that through these relationships, we would be able to learn deeply from one another and that I would come to know and love the people of Kosovo more deeply.

04

Eyes to See

Pray that I would be attentive to the Spirit and have eyes to see what the Lord is doing in Kosovo—that I would recognize where He is moving and faithfully join Him there.

“I REMAIN CONFIDENT OF THIS: I WILL SEE THE GOODNESS OF THE LORD IN THE LAND OF THE LIVING.”Psalm 27:13
